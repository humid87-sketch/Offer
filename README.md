# Lock Your Rate — August 2026 recovery offer

One offer, one list, ten days. Analysis of sales, membership and first-visit data
through 10 Aug 2026 to answer: what single offer collects the most cash this month
from clients who are still here or coming back.

- `docs/offer-strategy.md` — the recommendation and the evidence behind it
- `docs/scripts.md` — the call and WhatsApp scripts
- `data/lock-your-rate-call-sheet.csv` — 135 ranked names with the exact ticket for each
- `analysis/` — the scripts that produced the numbers

**The offer:** prepay six months of your current tier at today's rate. Seventh month
free. Classes roll over for the whole term. One 30-day freeze for travel.

**Addressable:** AED 313,650 from 66 live members, AED 355,350 from 69 summer-lapsed.
**Central case:** AED 95,000–100,000 collected in ten days.
