# Revision after Krysha's outreach results

**Supersedes the forecast and the term in `offer-strategy.md`.** The diagnosis in that
document still stands. The conversion model in it does not.

---

## What I got wrong

I read the five rate-lock sales of 1–10 August as *organic* — an unpromoted product
converting at 7.2% with nobody calling anyone — and used that as a floor to argue a
called campaign would reach 30%, or AED 98,000.

They were not organic. They were Krysha's campaign. The actual results:

| | |
|---|---|
| Contacted | 187 |
| Converted | **16** |
| Collected | **AED 38,150** |
| Reported conversion | **9%** |

My central case was 3.3× too high, and it was built on a floor that never existed.

---

## What the results actually say

### 1. The mechanic works. The term was wrong.

Of the nine rate-lock conversions, **eight took three months. One took six.**

| Offer taken | People | Collected | Avg |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3-month rate lock | 8 | AED 18,600 | AED 2,325 |
| Quarter Membership (4 Class) | 4 | AED 6,800 | AED 1,700 |
| Other upgrades | 3 | AED 4,350 | AED 1,450 |
| **6-month rate lock** | **1** | AED 8,400 | AED 8,400 |

Rate-lock prepay produced **AED 27,000 of the AED 38,150 — 71% of everything collected.**
The instrument is validated. I made six months the headline and three the fallback;
under a real push, **89% of people chose three.** That was backwards. Lead with three.

Note also that **4 Class – 3 Months (AED 1,650) and Quarter Membership – 4 Class
(AED 1,700) are the same product priced AED 50 apart.** Collapse them into one SKU.

### 2. The bottleneck is contact, not the offer

| | People | Converted | Rate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Actually spoke to them | 85 | 12 | **14.1%** |
| Unanswered WhatsApp | 102 | 4 | **3.9%** |
| Blended — the "9%" | 187 | 16 | 8.6% |

**Only 45% of the list was ever reached.** Being spoken to converts 3.6× better than
being messaged. The 9% headline is not the offer's conversion rate — it is the offer's
conversion rate diluted by a 55% miss rate. Fix the phone, not the price.

### 3. The refusals are about commitment, not money

| Reason given | People |
|---|---|
| **Staying on monthly** | **15** |
| Relocation / leaving the UAE | 13 |
| Pregnant or postnatal | 7 |
| Price | 6 |
| Planning to terminate or downgrade | 2 |

Price was the fifth reason, not the first. The top objection is the lock itself — which
is the same thing the playbook already flagged and never explained: *"nine long standing
members declined an offer and said they preferred monthly. We still do not know why."*
It has now happened again, to fifteen people, and it is still unexplained.

These fifteen are among the most consistent, highest-value members on the list. **Do not
re-pitch them this month.** Ask the question the playbook has been asking for: what do
they get from monthly that a locked rate takes away? Log it against `#money` and `#intent`.
