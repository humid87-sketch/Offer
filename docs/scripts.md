# Scripts

Calm, warm, precise. No exclamation marks. Never lead with the price.
Lead with the fact that they have been consistent, because that is both true and the
reason they are being offered it.

---

## The call — Pool 1 (still here)

> Hi [name], it's [you] from Kaizen. Nothing's wrong — I was looking at who's been most
> consistent with us this year and you came up. You've been on the [Eight] since [month],
> that's [11] months straight.
>
> We're doing one thing before the end of August, only for members who've been steady like that.
>
> There are two ways to do it. Six months of your [Eight] at the rate you're on — [AED 1,000],
> locked — and I put [8] extra classes on straight away, plus two guest passes so you can bring
> someone. Your classes stop expiring at the end of the month for the whole six, you get two
> 30-day freezes for travel, and booking opens to you 48 hours early.
>
> That's [AED 6,000] today. And if you end up moving, or you're pregnant, or you get injured,
> whatever's left comes back to you as credit — you're not stuck with it.
>
> Want me to set it up before the end of the month?

**If they raise travel, a possible move, or a pregnancy:** *"That's covered — if you leave the
country, fall pregnant or get injured, whatever's left comes back to you as credit. You're not
stuck with it."* This is the single most useful sentence in the script: 48% of August's refusals
were exactly this worry.

**Only if they've already said yes, and only for Simona Musso or Asmaa Chraibi:**
*"You can also lock six instead of three — same rate either way. Want me to do that?"*
Never before the yes, never to a hesitant one.

**If they mention travel:** *"That's actually why the freeze is in it. When are you away?
I'll note it now and we'll pause it for you."* — then log `#travel` with the dates.

**If it's a no:** *"That's completely fine. Can I ask — is it the timing or the commitment?"*
Write the answer down verbatim, tagged `#intent` or `#money`. Do not push twice.

## What not to say

- Do not mention the cafe, the sauna, recovery rituals or retail. 3.4% of revenue between
  them, and each one hands over a reason to say no.
- Do not offer a discount, and do not offer a free month. All sixteen August conversions paid
  the exact plain multiple of their own rate. This base buys class products at full price and
  has now done it twice. The concession is the exit, not the price.
- Do not offer a pack to someone already on a membership. They repurchase their own tier
  85% of the time; a pack is a product change dressed up as an upgrade.
- Do not extend past 31 August. The deadline is what makes it work.

## Log every outcome

Fill `outcome` and `notes` on the call sheet as you go. Use the playbook's eight tags:

`#goal` `#injury` `#travel` `#intent` `#prefers` `#social` `#money` `#life`

The `#intent` lines are the point. Twelve of them turned into six sales last time.
Every "call me in September" logged today is next month's list.
