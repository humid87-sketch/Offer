## Why this offer, and not a pack

The target list recommends a Twelve Pack at AED 1,700 to almost everyone. The data
says that is the wrong instrument, for four reasons.

**1. Members repurchase their own tier, they do not move.**
Across 4,242 repeat purchases, when someone buys again they buy the *same* product
85% of the time. Four Classes → Four Classes 85%. Eight → Eight 85%. Twelve → Twelve 79%.
Only 5% ever step up a tier. A pack asks a monthly member to change product. Rate-lock
asks them to change nothing except the date they pay.

**2. It is already selling, right now, at full price, with no campaign behind it.**
In the first ten days of August, five people bought a rate-lock prepay:

|---|---|---|---|
| 9 Aug | Weiner, Danica | 12 Class — 6 Month Rate Lock | AED 8,400 |
| 9 Aug | Alshareef, Roofy | 8 Class — 3 Month Rate Lock | AED 3,000 |

| 10 Aug | Rhodes, Rebecaa | 4 Class — 3 Month Rate Lock | AED 1,650 |
| 10 Aug | Ylitalo, Maria | 4 Class — 3 Month Rate Lock | AED 1,650 |

**AED 16,350 — 41% of everything collected in August so far, from five people.**
Every one of them was a consistent monthly member on that exact tier, and every one
paid the plain multiple of their own rate. Zero discount. Nobody called them.
That is a 7.2% conversion rate on a cold, unpromoted product in the deadest month
of the year. It is the strongest signal in the dataset.

**3. Packs are what burned the goodwill in the first place.**
629 classes have expired unused across the 211 people on the target list — AED 78,625
of paid-for value that evaporated. Selling another expiring pack to the people it
happened to repeats the injury. Rollover is the fix, and it costs nothing to give.

**4. The ticket is four to five times larger.**
Twelve Pack: AED 1,700. Six-month lock on the Eight tier: AED 6,000. Same phone call,
same person, same yes.

## The three things you said, checked against the data

**"Very few value the cafe items and recovery."** Correct, and it is not close.
Of AED 3.34M lifetime revenue: cafe AED 21,490 (**0.6%**), recovery and infrared
AED 92,320 (**2.8%**). Classes and memberships are **84%**. Every discount the studio
has ever given — 711 of them — was on a coffee, a Humantra box or a pair of socks.
**A class product has never been discounted.** That is an asset. Do not spend it now.

**"Driven by classes only."** Correct. Put nothing else in this offer. No sauna, no
smoothie, no tote. Every add-on gives them a reason to say "I don't really use that,"
and hands them the objection for free.

**"Only if they are consistently attending."** Correct, and it is the strongest
predictor in the data:

| Visits since first class | Clients | Average lifetime spend |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | 798 | AED 198 |
| 3–5 | 158 | AED 1,239 |
| 6–11 | 191 | AED 2,041 |
| 12–24 | 143 | AED 3,535 |
| **25+** | **199** | **AED 10,290** |

A consistent attender is worth 5× someone who came 6–11 times. This is why the call
sheet is ranked by months paid and recency, not by lifetime spend. **Sell to the habit,
not to the wallet.** The person who has paid you eleven months running at AED 1,000 is
a better call than the person who once spent AED 17,000 and has not been seen since March.

And it is why rollover matters more than a discount: rollover removes the one thing
that punishes a consistent attender for a bad week. Members already told you this —
*"I keep losing classes at the end of the month"* is quoted verbatim in the playbook.

## The one caveat, stated plainly

**This is pulled-forward cash, not new revenue.** Roughly AED 98,000 collected in August
is AED 98,000 that will not arrive as autopay between September and February, and the
bonus month is real margin given up.

It is still the right call, because the base went from 120 members to 54 in six months.
Cash from a member who might churn in October is worth materially more than the promise
of six more autopays from a member who will not be there. You are converting churn risk
into banked cash, and buying six months of guaranteed attendance from exactly the people
whose consistency drives everything else.

What it is not is a substitute for fixing acquisition. First visits have collapsed from
126 in September 2025 to 10 in the first ten days of August 2026, and the Referral Type
field is filled on **0.9%** of clients, so nobody can say from where. This offer buys the
autumn. It does not fix the top of the funnel — and it should not be asked to.
